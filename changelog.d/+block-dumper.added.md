Added `BlockDumper` (`abstract_block_dumper.v1.dumper`) for block tasks bound to queues with time limits, message TTL, throttling and a retry policy; its tasks are retried when their worker dies mid-execution. Added the `block_dumper_queues_v1` and `block_dumper_give_up_v1` commands, `manage.py check` warnings, queue metrics, and `MockBrokerContact` with `use_broker_contact` (`abstract_block_dumper.v1.testing`) for tests. Requires RabbitMQ and Celery's prefork pool. Migrations 0003-0006 alter `TaskAttempt`; on PostgreSQL their index builds block writes to that table while they run.
